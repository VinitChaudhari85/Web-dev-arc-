2025 Valorant Champions Tour: Toronto Masters

The 2025 Valorant Champions Tour: Toronto Masters, also known as Valorant Masters Toronto 2025, is an upcoming global tournament organized by Riot Games for the first-person shooter game Valorant as part of the Valorant Champions Tour 2025 competitive season. The tournament will be held between June 7-22 in Toronto, Canada. The top three teams of each regional league of Stage 1 competed in the tournament, which included the three International Leagues (Americas, EMEA, Pacific) and the China League. T1, the winners of Masters Bangkok in March, will not be able to defend their title as they were eliminated during Stage 1 in Pacific and did not qualify for the event.

Qualification

The top 3 stage 1 teams from NA, EMEA, APAC, CN Leagues will qalify for the 2025 masters toronto event. The stage 1 winner teams wil receive a head start and will head straight to the playoffs while the remaining 8 teams will have to compete in swiss stage to advance to playoffs.

Stages & Format

Swiss Stage

First up is swiss stage. The Second seed from the stage 1 will face the third seeds, after that the teams will be drawn against the teams with the same reecord. Teams that lose 2 times will be eliminated and the surviving teams will advance to the playoffs.

The opening matchups for the swiss stage have been drawn:

  • Gen.G vs. MIBR
  • Bilibili Gaming vs. Team Liquid
  • Sentinels vs. Wolves Esports
  • Team Heretics vs. Paper Rex

We have some interesting matchups, two star duelists aspas and t3xture will go head to head for the first time in valorant. After qualifying for its first international event of the year, BiliBili Gaming will play Team Liquid. Sentinels, after just coming up short in the Americas Stage 1 grand final, will go up against international debutants Wolves Esports. Finally, regional powerhouses looking to return to their former glory will square off in just their second-ever meeting when Team Heretics plays Paper Rex. The first meeting between the two teams was the in the Swiss Stage of Masters Madrid which resulted in a heated battle, giving us one of the best BO3s.

Playoffs

Regional champions G2 Esports, Xi Lai Gaming, Rex Regum Qeon, and FNATIC earned themselves a bye into the playoffs, eagerly awaiting the top four of the Swiss stage.
